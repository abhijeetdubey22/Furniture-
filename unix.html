Philosophical Foundation

The Art of Ébénisterie

We reject mass fabrication. Every creation follows a 300-year-old tradition of master joinery, where timber expands and breathes naturally through seasonal humidity shifts.

Chapter 01 • Material Respect

Conscious Seasoning

Raw Appalachian and French timbers are dried in solar kilns for eighteen months. We stabilize each slab to a precise 7% moisture equilibrium to guarantee that joints will never warp or split.

Chapter 02 • Structural Purity

Zero-Fastener Joinery

Our master cabinetmakers employ blind mortise-and-tenons, sliding dovetails, and organic wooden dowels. Eliminating steel screws prevents micro-stresses and allows furniture to endure over centuries.

Chapter 03 • Tactile Soul

Organic Botanical Finishes

Surfaces receive seven coats of cold-pressed walnut oil and alpine beeswax rubbed in with horsehair brushes. The surface feels warm and skin-like, with zero synthetic polyurethane coatings.
